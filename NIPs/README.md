# Nockchain Improvement Proposals (NIPs)

The goals of the NIP project are to:

- Make core development decision-making visible and open to entry.
- Standardize and provide high-quality documentation for Nockchain itself and conventions built upon it.

This repository tracks past and ongoing improvements to Nockchain in the form of Nockchain Improvement Proposals (NIPs). [NIP-0001](./NIPS/NIP-0001.md) governs how NIPs are published.

NIPs can be divided into the following categories:

- **Standards Track NIPs** are improvements to Nockchain that affect the protocol, consensus mechanisms, transaction validation, block production, networking, or other core components of the blockchain.
- **Informational NIPs** describe a Nockchain design issue, or provide general guidelines or information to the Nockchain community, but do not propose new features. Informational NIPs do not necessarily represent a Nockchain community consensus or recommendation, so users and implementors are free to ignore Informational NIPs or follow their advice.
- **Process NIPs** describe a process surrounding Nockchain, or proposes a change to (or an event in) a process. Process NIPs are like Standards Track NIPs but apply to areas other than the Nockchain protocol itself. They may propose an implementation, but not to Nockchain's core codebase; they often require community consensus; unlike Informational NIPs, they are more than recommendations, and users are typically not free to ignore them. Examples include procedures, guidelines, changes to the decision-making process, and changes to the tools or environment used in Nockchain development. Any meta-NIP is also considered a Process NIP.
